## Negative control

At gamma=0 there is no greedy-wrongness, so the task is greedy-solvable and
adaptive traversal should confer no advantage. The arms were required to agree
within 1 percentage point in the gated `hops_2_4` stratum.

| | DIRECT | TRAV | Gap | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| exact-set, gamma=0, `hops_2_4`, 3 seeds | 7/25,707 = 0.03% | 7,330/25,707 = 28.51% | **28.49pp** | 1.00pp |

The control is failed by more than 28 times its threshold.

## Why the comparison is not reportable

The gap is not evidence that adaptive traversal beats one-shot scoring. It is
evidence that one arm trained and the other did not.

**DIRECT collapsed to a constant prediction.** In the inspected run it emitted
class 0 on 14,991 of 15,000 holdout episodes — 99.94% — and produced only five
distinct classes across the entire holdout. Every anomaly in its summary follows
from that single fact: `class_only_accuracy` of 0.2501 is exactly the 25% abstain
base rate, and exact-set accuracy of 0.0002 sits roughly 300× *below* the 1/15
chance level, because a model that never emits a valid mask cannot be right by
accident.

A model that merely failed to learn would sit at chance. DIRECT sits far below
it, which is the signature of a degenerate constant predictor rather than a weak
one.

**TRAV did not behave like a trained model either.** Its accuracy across gamma is
28.51%, 38.01%, 6.85%, 24.91%, 23.11%. The preregistered hypothesis was that the
gap grows with measured greedy-wrongness. Instead the series lurches, with
gamma=0.2 falling to roughly a fifth of the gamma=0.1 value and no monotone
structure at all. That is the signature of unstable optimisation, not of a
learned traversal policy.

## Budget selection

B=128 was selected from DIRECT target-in-pool coverage in the gated stratum —
30.96%, 53.82%, 79.64%, 100.00% at B=16, 32, 64, 128 — recorded in the P0 report
before any accuracy existed and bound into the code freeze. Selection was not
informed by any result on this page.

## What was not done

No threshold, arm, seed, metric, schedule, or optimiser was adjusted in response
to these numbers, and none will be under this freeze. Per-arm adaptation after
seeing holdout results is what the preregistration forbids, and the holdout is
now spent: it was opened once, under freeze `07d18b39…`, and cannot be reused.

Any genuine retry requires a new generation, a fresh seven-gate P0 report, and a
new holdout. It is a new experiment, not a continuation of this one.

## Open diagnosis, not acted upon

The likely cause of DIRECT's collapse is that `update_count` is 1,172, a single
epoch over one 150,000-episode bucket, while DIRECT's 128 examined edges come
from a query-**independent** structural BFS pool. Its learning signal is
correspondingly weaker, and it appears to have settled into the majority-class
optimum that predicting abstain provides at a 25% abstain rate. TRAV selects its
own edges and evidently escaped that basin, though not into stable learning.

This is a hypothesis. It was not tested, because testing it by changing the
training budget after seeing holdout results is precisely the adaptation the
preregistration prohibits. It is recorded so that a future experiment can be
designed against it in advance.
